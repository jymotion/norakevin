FAQ

When and where is the wedding?

Saturday, September 18, 2027, in Philadelphia, PA. The ceremony, cocktail hour and reception will be at The Down Town Club.

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony starts at 4:30pm. Please plan to arrive 15–20 minutes early to find your seat. The full timeline is on the Schedule page.

When do I need to RSVP by?

Please RSVP by August 18 on the RSVP page. Search for your name and you can answer for everyone in your household at once.

Can I bring a guest?

We’re not able to accommodate guests that haven’t been formally invited. If you feel someone has been left off your invite, please feel free to reach out to us.

Are kids welcome?

Outside of the family members involved in the ceremony, this will be an adults-only event.

What should I wear?

Formal attire: Dark-colored suits for men (tie optional) and a floor-length gown, a fancy cocktail dress or a dressy pantsuit for women.

Will the wedding be indoors or outdoors?

The entire event will be indoors. The walk from our recommended hotels to the venue and the walk from the venue to the after-party are both under a half-mile.

Is there parking?

There is a garage around the corner from the venue at 615 Chestnut St, as well as others throughout the city. See the Hotels page for parking options specific to where you’re staying.

Where should I stay?

See the Hotels page for two hotels a short walk from the ceremony and reception, with room rates and booking links.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We’ll have two professional photographers at the Ceremony, so we’ll kindly ask everyone to put their phones away and refrain from taking their own.

Who should I contact with questions?

Prior to the event weekend, you can text Nora at 631-742-6881 or Kevin at 610-952-0884.
